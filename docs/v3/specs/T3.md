# T3 Desktop-app audio and its states
Frames: V3Start ("Where is the meeting?", microphone checkbox, steps text), V3NoSound, V3States "Live · screen sharing
stopped". Rows: FRAMES.md V3Start (not the calendar card), V3NoSound.
Paths: screens/Start.tsx, screens/NoSound.tsx (new), entrypoints/offscreen/main.ts, lib/audio/appAudio.ts (new),
lib/capture.ts, entrypoints/background.ts (wiring), router, tests.
- "In an app": on Start recording call `getDisplayMedia({video:true,audio:true,systemAudio:'include'})` from the side
  panel (if Chrome refuses there, from a helper tab like entrypoints/permission). Stop video tracks immediately.
  No audio track → show V3NoSound. Pass the audio stream to the offscreen recorder as the "others" source
  (same chunking, silence skip and continuous recording as tab audio).
- Silence watch: others level silent ≥10 s while mic has sound → `app-audio/silent` → V3NoSound. Track `ended`
  → `app-audio/ended` → Live banner "Rekord can’t hear <App> anymore". "Share again" repeats the picker and
  resumes the same meeting. "Keep going with just my microphone" continues mic-only.
- Meeting.source = 'app'. App name: event.app from T6 if present, else the words "the meeting app".
- Tests: silence watch timing (fake timers), source selection, ended → banner state.
