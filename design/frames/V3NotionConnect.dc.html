Connect Notion

Rekord connects to Notion with a secret you create once. It takes about two minutes.

  1. 1 In Notion, create a connection named Rekord. Open Notion connections
  2. 2 Copy its secret and paste it below.
  3. 3 On the Notion page where meetings should go, open ··· then Connections, and add Rekord.
Using Penv Cloud? Add it there as NOTION_TOKEN and Rekord picks it up.
Connect Rekord can only see the pages you add it to.
