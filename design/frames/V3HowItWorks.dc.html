How Rekord handles your meetings

  1. 1 While you talk, the meeting’s audio goes to your transcription service and comes back as text.
  2. 2 Your transcript and notes are saved in this browser, on this computer.
  3. 3 When Rekord writes your summary or answers a question, it sends the transcript to your assistant service.
